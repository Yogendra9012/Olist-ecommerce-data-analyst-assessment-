LOOKER STUDIO DASHBOARD SPECIFICATION

PAGE: Executive Overview

CANVAS:
16:9 landscape

TOP:
Title: Olist E-Commerce Business Performance Dashboard
Subtitle: Sales | Customer | Delivery | Satisfaction

FILTER BAR:
1. Purchase Date
2. Customer State
3. Product Category
4. Payment Type
5. Repeat Customer

KPI ROW:
1. Delivered Orders -> COUNT_DISTINCT(order_id)
2. Revenue -> SUM(revenue)
3. Average Order Value -> SUM(revenue) / COUNT_DISTINCT(order_id)
4. Average Review Score -> AVG(review_score)
5. On-Time Delivery Rate -> AVG(on_time) * 100
6. Repeat Customer Rate -> customer-level repeat rate; preferably pre-calculated in source or shown as a separate retention KPI

CHART 1:
Title: Monthly Revenue Trend
Chart: Time series / line
Dimension: month or order_purchase_timestamp by month
Metric: SUM(revenue)

CHART 2:
Title: Monthly Orders Trend
Chart: Time series / line
Dimension: month
Metric: COUNT_DISTINCT(order_id)

CHART 3:
Title: Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue
Chart: Horizontal bar
Dimension: product_category
Metric: SUM(revenue)
Sort: Descending
Row limit: 10

CHART 4:
Title: Revenue by Customer State
Chart: Horizontal bar
Dimension: customer_state
Metric: SUM(revenue)
Sort: Descending

CHART 5:
Title: Delivery Performance vs Review Score
Chart: Column/bar
Create calculated dimension:
CASE
WHEN delivery_vs_estimated_days <= 0 THEN "On time / early"
WHEN delivery_vs_estimated_days <= 7 THEN "1-7 days late"
ELSE ">7 days late"
END
Metric: AVG(review_score)

CHART 6:
Title: Payment Method Usage
Chart: Bar
Dimension: payment_type
Metric: COUNT_DISTINCT(order_id)
Sort: Descending

CHART 7:
Title: Revenue vs Freight Cost
Chart: Scatter
X-axis: freight
Y-axis: revenue
Dimension/detail: order_id

TABLE:
Title: Category Performance
Dimensions: product_category
Metrics: revenue, order_items, avg_item_price, freight
Sort by revenue descending

DESIGN RULES:
- Keep the dashboard management-focused.
- Avoid unnecessary charts.
- Use consistent number formatting.
- Use a clear hierarchy: KPIs -> trends -> drivers -> customer experience.
- Add a short text box stating that the analysis is based on historical Olist data and is observational.
